# Credits

## Art
- **Crop items and seed bags** come from the [Farming Set (Pixel-Art)](https://opengameart.org/content/farming-set-pixel-art) by ScratchIO (CC0 1.0). The source files are in `assets/cc0-farming-set/`. The turnip is a recoloured beet.
- **Everything else** is original 32x32 pixel art made for FarmLife with `tools/make_art.py`. That includes the ground, crops in the field, trees, shrubs, flower patches, buildings and houses, vehicles, tools, the player characters and the villagers (`assets/characters/villager_*.png`).
- Earlier versions used Kenney's Tiny Town and Tiny Farm (CC0 1.0). Thanks, [Kenney](https://www.kenney.nl)!
